## `> whoami`

<img align="right" width="280" src="https://octodex.github.com/images/securitocat.png" alt="Securitocat Octocat" />

I’m a technology enthusiast who enjoys building cool things, breaking them, and making them better. My work sits at the intersection of **Web3, full-stack engineering, intelligent systems, and connected hardware**. I care about scalable architecture, thoughtful interfaces, and products that solve real-world problems.

I’m equally happy exploring a new framework, tuning hardware, debugging a stubborn system, or gaming with a playlist in the background. My working philosophy is simple: **debug like a detective, code like an artist, and ship like it’s a feature.**

- **Current mission:** Build, break, optimize, and ship.
- **Curiosity fuel:** Web3, IoT, developer tools, intelligent systems, gaming, gadgets, and hardware.
- **Digital home:** [iamom.tech](https://www.iamom.tech/)
- **Open to:** Interesting collaborations, ambitious products, and conversations about technology.
